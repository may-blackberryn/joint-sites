Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Draft. This policy describes how the app works today and is pending legal review.

The short version

  • Your face photos are kept on your phone. They're sent to our server only to be analyzed or compared, and are never stored there.
  • We don't use your photos to identify you, and we don't sell or share your data for advertising. Dew has no ads, analytics or tracking.
  • An account is optional. If you create one, your results and check-ins sync, but your photos never do.
  • You can delete everything on your phone, and your account, from Settings at any time.

Who we are

Dew is an iPhone app that looks at a selfie and describes visible skin characteristics, such as dark marks, shine or visible pores, and suggests a skincare routine. In this policy, "we" means the team that makes Dew. You can reach us at hello@levelfitness.app.

How the analysis works

When you scan, Dew sends the photo over an encrypted connection to our server. The server passes it to an AI model from Anthropic (Claude), which returns a description of what's visible. The photo exists only in memory while the request runs, and our server doesn't save it. If you take a photo yourself or choose one from your library, a smaller Anthropic model first checks that it shows one face clearly.

When you compare two scans on Progress, the same thing happens with both photos. When Dew looks for patterns in your check-ins, it sends your findings, habit names and product names, never photos.

Anthropic processes this data for us as a service provider under its commercial terms, which don't allow it to train its models on this data. Like most providers, it may keep requests for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring.

Dew describes appearance only. It doesn't diagnose conditions, doesn't check moles, and isn't medical advice.

What we don't do

  • We don't create a faceprint or use your photo to recognize who you are.
  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We don't use advertising identifiers, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't save your photos on our server or let them sync to your account.

On your phone

Your photos and data are stored in Dew's private space on your iPhone, with the strongest file protection iOS offers, and are excluded from iCloud and device backups. They're not in your photo library unless you choose to save a timelapse there.

Who we share data with

  • Anthropic, to analyze photos and look for patterns, as described above.
  • Supabase, which hosts our server and, if you have an account, stores your account and synced data.
  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

Stores you open from a product link have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep data

  • Photos: not kept on our server at all.
  • Synced data (with an account): until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Server logs: request ids, timings and outcomes, never photos or results. Kept only as long as our hosting provider retains logs.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: Settings → Delete everything removes your photos and data from your phone, and from our server if you're signed in.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete account removes your account and everything synced to it.
  • Access or correct: email us and we'll help.

Depending on where you live (for example California, the EU or UK, or Mexico), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data. Email hello@levelfitness.app and we'll respond within the time the law requires.

Children

Dew isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has used Dew, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
